About the tournament

Built for clubs, made for players

Legends Cup Miami brings together academies and clubs from all over South Florida for a weekend of real competition, with professional tournament management from the first whistle to the awards ceremony.

  • U9 Future Stars to U19 divisions
  • Open to boys and girls teams
  • Minimum 3 games guaranteed
  • World Cup–style pool play
  • Trophies and medals for 1st & 2nd
  • Played under FYSA rules

"We love being a tournament with purpose for every club." Every team, from the youngest Future Stars to U19, plays at least three games and competes for the cup.

Check-in & eligibility
  • Open to properly registered youth teams from all U.S. Soccer–affiliated organizations (USYS, US Club, FYSA…).
  • Upload player passes, medical releases and the official roster to your GotSport team account before the event.
  • Players may play for one team only. Passes are checked against the approved roster before every game.
  • Up to 5 guest players per team, from the same membership platform (USYS or US Club).
Game day
  • Arrive at the field 30 minutes before kick-off for pass verification.
  • Unlimited substitutions at any stoppage, with the referee's permission.
  • No deliberate heading for U12 and younger.
  • Hydration break at the midpoint of each half when it's 85 °F or hotter.
  • Matching numbered jerseys; the home team (listed first) changes if colors conflict.
Points & tie-breakers
  • Win = 3 · Tie = 1 · Loss = 0. World Cup–style pool play.
  • Tie-breakers: head-to-head, goal differential (max ±4 per game), fewest goals allowed, most goals scored (max 4 per game), penalty kicks.
  • No overtime. Ties in elimination games go straight to penalty kicks.
Conduct, forfeits & refunds
  • Teams and supporters sit on opposite sidelines. Coaches are responsible for their spectators.
  • A red card means missing the next tournament game.
  • Not showing up with the minimum number of players is a 4-0 forfeit.
  • No refunds after acceptance, except if the tournament is cancelled or a team can't be bracketed.
Awards
Champions and finalists of every division receive team trophies and individual medals right after the final.
